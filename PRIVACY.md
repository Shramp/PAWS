# PAWS Privacy Policy

PAWS does not collect, transmit, or share any data.

- Everything you enter is stored in a database on your device and never leaves it unless you export it yourself.
- The app makes no network requests. There are no accounts, analytics, crash reporting, or advertising.
- Export and import use the system share sheet and file picker. Where those files go is entirely up to you.
- Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Shramp/PAWS/issues.
